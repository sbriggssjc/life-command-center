## PAGE 15 — Advancing American Kidney Health Initiative

**Left column — "KEY POLICY LEVERS SINCE 2019" — replace the ETC, KCC, and payment-update entries:**

**ETC Model (CMS) — TERMINATED.** CMS ended the mandatory ESRD Treatment Choices Model early, effective December 31, 2025 (finalized in the CY2026 ESRD PPS rule), after evaluations showed no significant improvement in home dialysis or transplant waitlisting and increased spend. Facilities and nephrologists returned to standard Medicare rates in 2026.

**KCC Model (CMS) — RESTRUCTURED & EXTENDED.** The KCF option was terminated; the CKCC options were restructured for PY2026 (74 participants) and extended one year through 2027. Changes include benchmark discounts, a reduced CKD quarterly capitation payment, and elimination of the kidney transplant bonus — a response to PY2023's ~$304M net Medicare loss despite quality gains (more optimal starts, higher home dialysis and living-donor activity).

**IOTA Model (CMS) — LIVE.** The mandatory Increasing Organ Transplant Access Model launched July 1, 2025 at 103 kidney transplant hospitals (six years, through mid-2031); Performance Year 2 began July 1, 2026 under the June 2026 final rule. IOTA is now the primary lever behind the "double kidneys by 2030" aim.

**Ongoing payment/quality updates.** ESRD PPS base rate $281.71 finalized for CY2026 (+2.2%); CY2027 proposed at $299.55, driven largely by phosphate-binder inclusion (~$15.96). ESRD QIP continues to tie a portion of facility payment to quality.

### WHERE THE GOALS STAND IN 2026 (WHAT'S ACTUALLY HAPPENING)

**The "80% by 2025" home/transplant goal came due — and fell well short.** Incident home-modality use roughly doubled over the decade (8.5% in 2012 to ~14.5% in 2022), and incident peritoneal dialysis reached an all-time high of 14.1% in 2023 (USRDS 2025 ADR) — real progress, but a fraction of the aspiration. With ETC terminated for lack of effect, home-modality momentum now rides on KCC, operator programs, and payment design rather than a mandatory model.

**ESKD incidence keeps trending down.** Adjusted incidence fell ~18.6% over the decade through 2022, supporting AAKH's 2030 reduction goal — with GLP-1s an emerging incremental tailwind. Prevalence (831,192 at end-2023) held flat for the first time since 2019.

**Transplant access reform has shifted to IOTA.** HRSA/OPTN modernization plus the mandatory IOTA model (103 hospitals, PY2 underway) are now the levers to raise waitlisting and living-donor transplants.
