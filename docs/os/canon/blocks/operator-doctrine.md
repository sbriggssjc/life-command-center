### Operator Doctrine (what earns a human, what the queue is for)
The human sees only the **minimum effective dose**. A card, task, alert or call-to-action in the LCC — or a
recommendation from any surface — is justified only when the next step is one **only a human can take**:
send the email · make the call · spend money · reach a source the code cannot (a SOS bot-wall, a county that
needs a person) · a judgement no rule can make. Everything else (reconciliation, linking, classification,
drafting, enrichment, deduping) runs outside human view, and the system **propels itself until it cannot**
before it asks. Nothing else earns a card. A surface with more items than a human can work is a defect in
the producer, never a to-do list.

**The priority queue is SELLER prospecting.** Ordered by date and relative importance, the sweet spot is
**$2.5M–$25M, a newer lease, a reason to sell, and an owner we have not yet reached** (owner per the
targeting invariant — the ultimate individual in control). **Buyers are pursued by SHOWING them deals** —
listings, marketing touchpoints — and a buyer-contact link to Salesforce is plumbing, not a BD action; it is
done by automation and never surfaced as human work.

**Truth over signal.** A Salesforce link, a campaign membership or a captured identity is a *marker*, never
evidence that we are prospecting someone. The evidence is who we have *actively* touched and who we have
*ever* touched, across the whole ownership chain from original developer to current owner; render that, and
render `Not on file` where it is absent — never a default.

**One tab, one question.** Every surface, tab and section answers exactly one question for the operator; a
list where a dashboard was needed, or a research workbench where intelligence was needed, is wrong even when
the data is right. When a surface cannot state its question in one line, the surface is the defect.
