# Dialysis Net-Lease Market Brief: Q3 2026
*Prepared for Scott Briggs, Northmarq | As of September 11, 2026 | Research draft. Figures marked [UNVERIFIED] need confirmation before external use.*

## Executive Summary
- **Fresenius is shrinking its U.S. footprint while DaVita holds steady.** In Q2 2026 FMC exited about 100 U.S. clinics, total clinics fell 4% to 3,513, and U.S. same-market treatments dropped 0.9%. Management guides to about the same level for the rest of 2026 [1][2]. DaVita reported normalized non-acquired treatment growth of +0.3%, 2,671 U.S. centers, and reaffirmed 2026 guidance [3]. On its call, DaVita said fewer admissions from closed Fresenius clinics partly offset its gains [4].
- **The CY2027 reimbursement headline overstates the real increase.** CMS proposed a base rate of $299.55, up $17.84 from $281.71, but total payments rise only about 1.1% for freestanding facilities. About $15.96 of the base-rate increase comes from folding phosphate binders into the bundle, not new margin [5][6]. The ETC model ended on 12/31/2025 [7].
- **Rates are the main headwind for pricing.** The 10-year Treasury was about 4.94% on 9/11/2026, near its highest level since 2023. Trading Economics reports markets pricing about a 90% chance of a Fed *hike* [8][9]. Boulder Group's overall STNL cap rate edged up to 6.82% in Q2 2026 (from 6.80% in Q1), and Boulder notes that expected 2026 Fed cuts are no longer on the table [10][11].
- **Credit gap between tenants.** Fresenius is investment grade (Baa3/BBB-, stable, per its May 2024 release) [12]. DaVita is BB+/Ba2, stable, per a third-party broker page [13]. One broker quotes DaVita NNN cap rates at 5.50%–6.75% [13]. I could not verify a published dialysis-specific cap rate average for 2026 from a research house.
- **Takeaway:** Buyers will underwrite each site on its own merits (market share, patient census, lease term, rent versus market) and will separate FMC sites at risk of closure from DaVita sites that are gaining patients. Sellers of long-term, well-performing clinics should move before any further rise in rates. Short-term FMC leases in overlapping markets face the widest bid-ask spreads.

## 2. Policy and Reimbursement
- **CY2027 ESRD PPS proposed rule (released 6/24/2026) [5][6]:**
  - Base rate $299.55, up from $281.71.
  - Total payments up 1.1% (freestanding 1.1%, hospital-based 2.0%). About $6.2B to about 7,600 facilities.
  - Market basket 1.6%.
  - $15.96 of the increase comes from moving phosphate binders out of TDAPA and into the base rate, plus a 6% operating-cost add-on.
  - Offset by a 0.98898 budget-neutrality factor tied to the expanded Low-Volume Payment Adjustment.
  - Comment deadline 8/25/2026. Final rule typically late October or November.
- **CY2026 final (for context):** base rate $281.71, up about 2.2% in total. ETC model terminated 12/31/2025 for missing its home-dialysis and savings targets [7].
- **IOTA:** a mandatory six-year transplant-hospital model that started 7/1/2025. It affects transplant access, not dialysis facility payment directly [15].
- **Medicare Advantage:** Avalere (3/31/2026) says ESRD enrollment in MA has grown to roughly match traditional fee-for-service [15]. Analyst view: this matters for payer-mix margins. I could not verify a precise current share.
- **GLP-1s:**
  - Reported: DaVita research (11/20/2025) linked GLP-1 use to 9% fewer hospitalizations and 17% lower mortality among ESKD patients [16].
  - Narrative (analyst opinion): GLP-1s may slow CKD progression over the long term (FLOW trial). In the near term, lower mortality supports census. DaVita credited lower mortality for part of its Q2 volume gains [4].
  - I found no operator quantifying lower dialysis demand from GLP-1s so far.

## 4. Implications for Sellers and Buyers (analyst opinion)
1. **Underwrite each clinic, not the brand.** With FMC closing about 100 U.S. clinics and guiding to negative same-market volume, buyers will check each FMC site's census, CMS star rating, and nearby competition. DaVita sites near closed FMC clinics may pick up patients and gain a pricing edge.
2. **Rate pressure outweighs reimbursement.** The CY2027 rule adds about 1.1% and is neutral for rent coverage. A 10-year near 4.9–5.0% with hike risk narrows positive leverage. Expect cap rates to hold flat or move slightly wider in Q4, not compress.
3. **The credit gap is widening in how buyers price it.** Investment-grade FMC credit still attracts 1031 and institutional buyers, but FMC's operating trend makes term and renewal likelihood more important than the rating. For DaVita (BB+), strong store-level performance drives value.
4. **Sellers should consider selling now.** Long-term, recently renewed dialysis leases with escalators remain liquid. The Bronx trade shows deep demand in infill locations. Waiting risks higher rates and further FMC optimization news.
5. **Buyers have an opening.** Short-term or FMC-overlap assets with wide bid-ask spreads offer yield for investors comfortable with re-leasing risk. Structure around renewal-option probability.
