## 1. Population, reconstructed — and why it is 92 orgs / 111 pairs, not 58 / 78

No saved query exists for this population (C2h §6 said the same), so it was rebuilt from scratch:
a gov property's `true_owner_effective_id` (via `lcc_property_owner_facts`) → the LCC entity carrying
that `gov/true_owner` external identity → that org has an `associated_with` edge to a person carrying a
`salesforce/Contact` identity → the property's `gov/asset` entity is resolved in `lcc_property_owner`
to **some other** entity → and the org is **never** the resolved owner of any asset.

| step | count |
|---|---:|
| gov facts with an anchored `true_owner` | 9,619 |
| owner-orgs with ≥1 Salesforce contact whose property resolved elsewhere | 188 orgs / 414 rows |
| …of which the org is resolved owner of **nothing** (the C2g bucket) | **92 orgs / 111 pairs** |
| …of which the org was never even a candidate in `lcc_property_owner_evidence` | 88 |

92 vs. the tracked 78: this join reaches Salesforce through `associated_with` (there are **0**
`works_at` edges live — §6's description of its own join cannot have used that type), and includes
multi-property orgs (`US Fed Properties Trust Inc` alone is 12 pairs). The shape matches C2h/§6 —
**`supersession` at a flat 0.75 on 76 of 111**, `relationship_graph` on 35 — so this is the same
population, read wider, not a different one. **78 stays the tracked number**; 92/111 is what was read.

## 3. Why LCC never shows the sponsor — the gate

`lcc_ingest_domain_owner_evidence` (P113) reads `v_lcc_domain_owner_candidates`. That view's second
CTE:

```sql
unresolved AS (
  SELECT ... FROM asset a_1
  WHERE NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM lcc_property_owner po
                    WHERE po.entity_id = a_1.entity_id AND po.owner_entity_id IS NOT NULL))
```

**The domain `true_owner` is only ever proposed for an asset with no resolved owner.** On the 92
assets read here, `lcc_property_owner_evidence` holds `gov_ownership_transition` (77, weight 1.0),
`rel_purchase` (20, 4.0) and `rel_owns` (9, 3.0) — and **zero** `domain_true_owner` rows, even though
the feeder's own weight for it is **5.0**, higher than any of them. The R6 doctrine "per-row domain
truth OUTRANKS name patterns" (`20260606121000_lcc_r6_owner_facts_and_resolution.sql`) is
implemented as *domain truth fills gaps*: it outranks nothing it never competes with.

`supersession-tie-lane-2026-08.md` §4 says "`domain_true_owner` already outranks `rel_purchase`
precisely because it is the curated beneficial owner." **That is true of the weights and false of
the flow** — on any asset `supersession` or `relationship_graph` reached first, the domain feeder
never ran at all, so its weight never mattered.

**This is not filed as a bug.** The gate reads as P113's design — domain truth shipped as a
gap-filler — though whether that was chosen or merely inherited is not established here. Whether it should
*also* be allowed to supersede is exactly the beneficial-owner-vs-SPE question — now with its real
size.

## 4. Blast radius of lifting the gate

Resolved assets whose domain `true_owner` maps to an LCC entity **different from** the resolved owner,
and which would pass the view's own eligibility checks (not operator, name promotable, not blocked):

| domain | resolved assets with domain truth | **would be eligible AND disagree** | agree | no owner entity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| gov | 9,394 | **936** | 8,434 | 23 |
| dia | 842 | **100** | 294 | 17 |

So the decision in `supersession-tie-lane-2026-08.md` §4 was sized on the 63 assets where a single
deed carries both `buyer` and `true_buyer`. **The same semantic choice, made through the domain
feeder, touches 1,036 assets.** Evidence quality across the gov 936 is not uniform: gov-wide, of
3,264 properties where `true_owner` ≠ `recorded_owner`, **978 have any SOS manager on the SPE and
858 of those (88%) name the `true_owner`** — the other ~2,300 are unattested, not contradicted.

**Not built.** Lifting `unresolved` is a one-line view change; what it changes is what
`lcc_property_owner` *means* (§4 of the tie-lane doc says this correctly). Two honest options and one
I would not take:

- **Keep the SPE, add the sponsor as a first-class edge** — the tie-lane doc's "cleaner model." Needs
  a relationship type LCC does not carry; the 43 A-class pairs are the seed set with registry
  evidence.
- **Let domain truth supersede** — flip the gate, let weight 5.0 win. 1,036 assets change meaning at
  once; a broker gets the sponsor. The 858 SOS-attested are safe; the unattested rest inherit gov's
  `true_owner` quality wholesale.
- ⛔ **Bulk-write the 43 into `lcc_ownership_sponsor_family`** — it is keyed `(sponsor, token)` and
  most A-class SPEs carry **no sponsor token** (`300 Fifth Avenue LLC` ← Martin Selig, `TREE NUTS, LLC`
  ← Paul Parreira). The surface cannot express a per-entity manager relationship; forcing it there
  would be the third detector C2h §4 warned against, wearing a confirm table's clothes.
